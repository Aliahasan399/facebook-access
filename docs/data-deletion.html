Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 04, 2026

How to Delete Your Data

This application does not permanently store any personal data. All API access tokens are stored locally on your machine.

To delete all data:

  1. Remove or revoke the Facebook access token from your app's Facebook Login settings
  2. Delete the application from your machine

To revoke the app's access to your Facebook account:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings
  2. Click "Apps and Websites"
  3. Find this app and click "Remove"
